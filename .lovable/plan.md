# Darker, softly blurred homepage gallery plan

## Goal
Keep the five-second homepage photo sequence, but make every background photograph subtly blurred and consistently darker so the headline, navigation, links, captions, and image count remain readable throughout the full cycle.

## Changes
- Apply a restrained, uniform blur to both the incoming and outgoing hero photographs so the crossfade never shifts between sharp and blurred states.
- Slightly enlarge the blurred image layer within the existing clipped hero area, preventing soft filter edges from appearing around the viewport.
- Strengthen the dark veil across the full hero, with extra protection behind the top navigation and lower text areas.
- Preserve the photographs' recognizability, existing crops, five-second timing, captions, counter, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep the `/gallery` photographs full-detail and unblurred; this treatment applies only to the homepage background.

## Verification
- Review multiple slides at desktop and phone widths, including the brightest photograph.
- Confirm all foreground words remain legible, the crossfade has no flashes or exposed edges, and no content overlaps.
- Confirm the gallery page remains visually unchanged and the project finishes with a clean build.

## Scope boundary
No changes to the contact form, photography order, captions, public routes, email delivery, or gallery-page image treatment.
